# 潜る · Moguru

*dive deep into Japanese, one comprehensible sentence at a time*

![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue.svg) ![Local-first](https://img.shields.io/badge/local--first-yes-green.svg) ![Built on MCP](https://img.shields.io/badge/built%20on-MCP-8A2BE2.svg)

You don't study a language. You dive into it.

Moguru is a private, local immersion companion for Japanese. It mines the right sentences from what you're already watching and reading, explains the grammar that's actually blocking you, and — with its **shadow model** — learns what you *truly* understand in native content, not just what you made flashcards for. It turns AJATT's black box into a glass box.

潜る means *to dive* — and 潜 also means *latent*: the hidden model of what you really know.

Runs on open-weight models. Your data never leaves your machine.
